AXA PPP is a major private health insurer, built on Azure and .NET, with eight agile teams. I spent my university industry year there, on two teams.

The teams

  • PACE: an in-house tool that records marketing consent across the business’s products. It has an AngularJS front end and a .NET API, with IBM MDM matching records for the same person. I was one of two .NET developers, and worked across the front end, the back end and fully automated BDD and unit tests.
  • PMA: the Provider Management Application, which manages information on providers, from small specialists to major private hospitals. It was built with .NET MVC and jQuery. Near the end of my year, I built a proof of concept showing how React could improve its performance and developer productivity.
